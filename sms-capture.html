iPhone Shortcuts setup

  1. Open Shortcuts and create a personal automation for Messages.
  2. Filter by sender or keywords like “debited”, “credited”, or “UPI”. Avoid forwarding OTPs.
  3. Add “Get Contents of URL” with POST to the capture endpoint.
  4. Request body JSON: {"message":"Message text","sender":"SMS sender"}
  5. Headers: Authorization: Bearer YOUR_TOKEN and Content-Type: application/json.
