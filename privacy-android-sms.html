Payables Tracker Android Notification Companion — Privacy Notice

Effective date: 22 November 2026

This notice explains the optional Android notification capture feature in Payables Tracker.

What the companion accesses

After you enter a device capture token, review the in-app disclosure, give affirmative consent, and enable Android Notification Access, Android can deliver notification content to this app. Notification Access is a broad operating-system capability. The companion's implementation checks the package name first and processes notification content only when it comes from Android's currently selected default SMS app. It does not request SMS inbox permission, read the SMS provider, or retrieve existing message history.

The feature can capture only notifications that the default SMS app actually posts and makes available. It cannot capture messages whose notifications are disabled, suppressed, hidden, or omitted by that app. Notification formats vary. Local filtering looks for transaction amounts and credit/debit language and attempts to exclude OTP/authentication messages; this is best-effort, not a guarantee.

What is transmitted and why

For a notification matching the local transaction filter, the companion sends the visible notification text and conversation title over HTTPS to the Payables Tracker SMS Capture service. This supports transaction capture, account mapping, duplicate detection, and Cash Book/review processing. Notification content is not used for advertising or sold.

On-device security and control

The device capture token and queued notification payloads are encrypted using Android Keystore-backed encryption. Network requests use HTTPS. Turn off consent in the companion or revoke its Notification Access in Android settings to stop future capture. Revoking or rotating the device token also prevents that token from authorizing future requests.

Already transmitted transaction data may remain in your Payables Tracker account until managed or deleted through the applicable account controls.

Service providers

Transaction data is sent to the Supabase-hosted Payables Tracker backend configured for your account. The backend performs authoritative parsing, duplicate checks, account mapping, and ledger/review processing.

Your choices

  • Do not enable the feature if you do not want this processing.
  • Turn off consent to stop future forwarding.
  • Revoke Notification Access in Android settings.
  • Revoke or rotate the device token in Payables Tracker.
  • Uninstall the companion to stop its operation.

Changes and contact

This notice may be updated when data practices change. For privacy questions or account-data requests, use the support/contact channel provided within your Payables Tracker account.
